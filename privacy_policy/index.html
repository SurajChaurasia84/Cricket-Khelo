1. Introduction

Welcome to Cricket Khelo. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy informs you about how we look after your personal data when you use our mobile application and tells you about your privacy rights.

2. Data We Collect

To provide a seamless experience for finding cricket matches and players, we collect the following information:

  • Identity Data: Full name and profile photo (retrieved via Google/Firebase Authentication).
  • Contact Data: Email address used for account creation.
  • Location Data: We collect your Precise GPS Location (Latitude and Longitude) to calculate the distance between you and other matches or players near you. This is essential for the core functionality of the app.
  • Social Data: Information you provide in match descriptions, availability requests, and in-app chat messages.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your player profile.
  • To display your distance to other users so you can find local matches.
  • To send you push notifications about match invites or chat messages via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
  • To facilitate real-time communication between captains and players.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party providers to power Cricket Khelo:

  • Google Firebase: Used for Authentication, Firestore database, Realtime Database (Chat), and Cloud Messaging (Notifications).
  • Google Maps/Geolocator: Used to determine your approximate location for match filtering.

5. Data Security

We have implemented appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way. All data is stored securely using Firebase's encrypted infrastructure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. You can do this by contacting us or using the logout/account options within the app.
