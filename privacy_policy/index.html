1. Introduction

Welcome to Cricket Khelo. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy informs you about how we handle your data when you use our mobile application.

2. Data We Collect

Cricket Khelo collects the following information to provide matchmaking and notification services:

  • Identity & Contact: We collect your name, email address, and profile picture when you sign in using Google.
  • Precise Location: We access your GPS Location to show you cricket matches and players nearby (within 10km). This is required for the core functionality of the app.
  • User Content: We store match invitations, player requests, and chat messages you send within the app.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services which may collect data according to their own policies:

  • Google Firebase: For authentication, database storage, and push notifications.
  • Unity Ads: To display advertisements within the app. Unity may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To find and display nearby cricket matches.
  • To send notifications about match invites or chat messages.
  • To facilitate communication between players and captains.
  • To provide a personalized user profile.

5. Data Security & Deletion

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase infrastructure. You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time through the "Delete Account" option in the My Profile screen.
